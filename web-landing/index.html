Why landing pages

One page, one job: convert.

When you're running a campaign, sending traffic to your homepage kills conversions, there's too much going on. A landing page is different: it's built around one offer and one action, with nothing to distract from it.

Every element, the headline, the copy, the button, works toward getting the visitor to convert. Done right, a good landing page can dramatically lift your campaign results.
