Why we're building these

Good marketing decisions start with good information, and most small teams don't have the budget for a stack of paid tools just to answer simple questions. How's my page doing on SEO? What should my ad budget be? Is this headline any good? You shouldn't need a subscription to find out.

So we're putting the checks we run internally into simple, free tools anyone can use. No account, no credit card, no email wall. Just open the tool, get your answer, and get on with your day. They're built for founders, marketers, and small business owners in Jaipur and across India who want clear answers without the fluff.

What to expect

Each tool does one job and does it well. You'll get a quick result you can actually act on, plus a short explanation of what it means and what to do next. No vanity scores, no jargon. If a tool tells you something's wrong, it'll also tell you how to fix it, or point you to someone who can.
